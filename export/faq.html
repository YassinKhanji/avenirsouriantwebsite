Frequently Asked Questions

What ages are your programs suitable for?

▼

Our programs are designed for children between the ages of 1 and 12, divided into three main groups: Toddlers (1-3), Preschool (3-5), and School Age (5-12).

Do I need prior experience to enroll my child?

▼

No prior experience is necessary for any of our programs! We offer introductory classes that teach the fundamentals in a fun, pressure-free environment.

What are your class sizes?

▼

We keep our class sizes small (typically 8-12 students per instructor) to ensure every child receives personalized attention and support.

Do you offer trial classes?

▼

Yes, we offer a free trial class for new families. Please contact us to schedule a visit and see our programs in action.

How can I register my child?

▼

You can register your child by clicking the 'Enroll Now' button on our website, or by visiting our center in person.
